Day-by-day itinerary

Your adventure with Travellite

1

Arrival in Entebbe, Uganda

Airport TransferHotel Stay

Welcome to Uganda. Meet at Entebbe International Airport and transfer to your hotel. Evening briefing about your gorilla tracking adventure.

2

Transfer to Bwindi Impenetrable Forest

8–9 hrs driveScenic Route

Early departure for Bwindi Impenetrable National Park through Uganda’s countryside, with stops at local villages and the equator. Evening arrival at your lodge near the forest.

3

Gorilla Tracking in Bwindi

2–6 hrs trekWildlife Viewing

Early briefing before entering the forest. Trek with expert guides to spend an unforgettable hour with a mountain gorilla family. Afternoon at leisure or optional community visit.

4

Transfer to Volcanoes National Park, Rwanda

Border CrossingScenic Drive

Cross into Rwanda and drive to Volcanoes National Park. Afternoon cultural experience at Iby’Iwacu Cultural Village or relax at your lodge with Virunga views.

5

Gorilla Tracking in Volcanoes National Park

2–5 hrs trekVolcano Slopes

Second gorilla tracking experience on Rwanda’s volcanic slopes. Compare habitats and families between Uganda and Rwanda. Celebration dinner in the evening.

6

Transfer to Kigali and Departure

2.5 hrs driveAirport Transfer

Morning visit to the Dian Fossey Gorilla Fund Center or Kigali Genocide Memorial (time permitting). Transfer to Kigali International Airport for your departure flight.
