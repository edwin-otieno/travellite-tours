Day-by-day itinerary

Your adventure with Travellite

1

Pick Up, Transfer to Masai Mara & Afternoon Game Drive

6 hrs driveAfternoon Game Drive

Day one takes you on a roughly 6-hour drive to Masai Mara, with a stop at the Great Rift Valley Viewpoint, a short break in Narok Town, check-in for lunch, and an afternoon game drive until late.

  • 6:00 am — Pick-up near Nairobi CBD or your residence, safari briefing, and departure.
  • 7:30 am — Photo stop at the Great Rift Valley Viewpoint.
  • Stop in Narok for a short stretch and last-minute supplies.
  • Arrive via Sekenani Gate, check in at camp along the Talek River, buffet lunch.
  • 3:00 pm — Afternoon game drive among giraffes, jackals, zebras, antelopes, and birds. Dinner and overnight at camp.
2

Full Day Game Drive with Packed Lunch

Full dayWildlife Viewing

Rise early for breakfast by 6 am and carry a packed lunch. Traverse the grasslands searching for lions, cheetahs, leopards, elephants, wildebeest, and more. Morning drives offer the best chance to see big cats on the hunt. Picnic in the bush, then continue toward the Mara River for hippos and crocodiles until dusk.

3

Depart Masai Mara for Nairobi

Morning Game DriveReturn to Nairobi

After a relaxed breakfast, check out and depart for Nairobi with a morning game drive en route. Drop-off in town or at your place of residence.
