4 Nights 5 Days Nairobi, Nakuru and Maasai Mara Package

A classic Kenya circuit combining Nairobi, the flamingo-lined shores of Lake Nakuru, and two full days in the world-famous Masai Mara. Spot rhinos at the soda lake, then seek the Big Five across Mara’s open plains — paced for travelers who want variety without a rushed schedule.

Day-by-day itinerary

Your adventure with Travellite

1

Arrival in Nairobi

Airport TransferCity Stay

Meet and greet at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport and transfer to your Nairobi hotel. Evening at leisure with an optional safari briefing.

2

Nairobi to Lake Nakuru

Rift ValleyFlamingos

Drive into the Great Rift Valley toward Lake Nakuru National Park. Afternoon game drive around the soda lake — renowned for flamingos, pelicans, and a rhino sanctuary. Overnight near the park.

3

Lake Nakuru to Masai Mara

Scenic TransferAfternoon Drive

After breakfast, continue south to Masai Mara National Reserve. Arrive for lunch, then an afternoon game drive among lions, elephants, zebra, and wildebeest. Dinner and overnight at camp or lodge.

4

Full Day in Masai Mara

Full Day SafariOptional Balloon

Full-day wildlife viewing with picnic lunch. Optional balloon safari at dawn (extra). Afternoon continue game drives or visit a Maasai community (optional). Overnight at camp.

5

Return to Nairobi

Morning DriveDrop-off

Morning game drive en route as you depart the Mara for Nairobi. Drop-off at your hotel or the airport for onward travel.
