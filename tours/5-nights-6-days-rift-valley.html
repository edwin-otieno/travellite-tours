Day-by-day itinerary

Your adventure with Travellite

1

Arrival in Nairobi

Airport TransferHotel Stay

Warm welcome on arrival at the airport with refreshments and safari essentials. Transfer to your Nairobi hotel for rest and overnight.

2

Aberdare National Park

Forest LodgeWaterhole Viewing

Drive north through farms, coffee, and pineapple country to Aberdares. Lunch at a country club, then transfer to a tree lodge deep in the forest. Enjoy floodlit waterhole viewing — elephants, rhino, buffalo, bushbuck, forest hog, and possibly leopard or rare bongo. Warm jacket and hiking boots recommended.

3

Lake Nakuru National Park

Great Rift ValleyFlamingos

Depart via scenic Rift Valley views of Mt Longonot and Lake Naivasha. Afternoon game drive around Lake Nakuru — famous soda lake birdlife and a rhino sanctuary. Dinner and overnight at camp or lodge.

4

Masai Mara National Reserve

Scenic TransferAfternoon Game Drive

Travel to the northern extension of the Serengeti plains — Masai Mara — home to black-maned lions, leopard, giraffe, elephant, rhino, zebra, and wildebeest. Late lunch, then afternoon game drive. Overnight at camp or lodge.

5

Full Day Masai Mara & Cultural Visit

Full Day AdventureMaasai Village

Full-day safari with lunch at camp. Visit a traditional Maasai Manyatta (off the beaten track) to learn customs and traditions. Optional balloon safari over the Mara plains with champagne breakfast.

6

Return to Nairobi

Return TransferAirport Drop-off

Breakfast, check-out, and farewell to the lodge team. Drive back to Nairobi with photographs of the journey. Drop-off at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport for your onward flight.
