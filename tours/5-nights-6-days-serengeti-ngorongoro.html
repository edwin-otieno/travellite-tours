5 Nights 6 Days Glimpse of Serengeti & the Ngorongoro

A focused Tanzania safari pairing endless Serengeti plains with the wildlife-rich floor of Ngorongoro Crater. Ideal for travelers who want iconic Big Five sightings, migration landscapes (seasonal), and crater-rim sunsets in under a week.

Serengeti PlainsNgorongoro CraterBig Five
Day-by-day itinerary

Your adventure with Travellite

1

Arrival in Arusha

Airport TransferHotel Stay

Meet at Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO) or Arusha Airport and transfer to your Arusha hotel. Safari briefing and overnight.

2

Arusha to Serengeti

Game Drive En RoutePlains

Drive toward Serengeti National Park via the Ngorongoro Conservation Area viewpoint. Afternoon game drive on arrival. Overnight in or near the Serengeti.

3

Full Day Serengeti

WildlifePhotography

Full day exploring central or seasonal migration corridors — lions, cheetahs, elephant herds, and vast plains game. Picnic lunch in the park. Overnight in Serengeti.

4

Serengeti to Ngorongoro

Crater RimScenic Drive

Morning game drive as you exit Serengeti toward Ngorongoro. Afternoon at leisure on the crater rim with sweeping views. Overnight at a rim lodge or camp.

5

Ngorongoro Crater Floor

Crater SafariBig Five

Descend into the crater for a full morning of wildlife viewing — dense concentrations of lion, buffalo, elephant, and often black rhino. Picnic lunch on the crater floor, then ascend to the rim. Overnight.

6

Return to Arusha & Departure

TransferAirport

Breakfast and drive back to Arusha. Drop-off at your hotel or airport for departure.
